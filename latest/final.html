1. CraftNote – AI Meeting Transcription

CraftNote turns audio from Zoom, Teams or in‑person recordings into searchable text and action items.

  1. Create a free account. Visit craftnote.com, click “Sign Up”, and choose the free plan (10 uploads/week).
  2. Connect your meeting platform. In the dashboard, go to Integrations → Zoom and follow the OAuth prompts. You should see a confirmation banner “Zoom linked”.
  3. Upload a recording. Click “New Transcription”, drag a .mp4 or .wav file, and press “Start”. After a few minutes the page shows a progress bar and then the transcript.
  4. Review speaker tags. CraftNote automatically labels speakers. If a name is missing, click the speaker icon and type the correct name – the AI will remember it for future meetings.
  5. Export or search. Use the “Export” button to download a .docx file, or type a question in the search bar (e.g., “What were the action items for project X?”) to get instant answers.

Tip

For offline meetings, download the CraftNote mobile app and record directly on your phone – the AI works without an internet connection.

2. Notion AI – Smart Document Organization

Notion AI adds generative features to the popular note‑taking workspace.

  1. Sign up for Notion. Go to notion.com and create a free workspace.
  2. Enable Notion AI. Inside any page, click the “AI” button on the top‑right toolbar. Choose “Enable Notion AI” and accept the terms.
  3. Summarize a long note. Highlight a paragraph, press the AI button, and select “Summarize”. The AI will replace the selection with a concise summary.
  4. Generate a to‑do list. Type “/ai” and choose “Generate checklist”. Provide a brief prompt like “Tasks for launching my freelance website”. The AI creates a ready‑to‑check list.
  5. Link to other pages. Use the AI to suggest related pages by typing “Find related notes about SEO”. Accept the suggested links to keep knowledge connected.

3. Claude – Long‑Form Writing Assistant

Claude (by Anthropic) excels at drafting essays, reports and marketing copy.

  1. Access Claude. Visit claude.ai and log in with your Google or Microsoft account.
  2. Start a new conversation. Click “New Chat”, then type a clear instruction, e.g., “Write a 500‑word article on remote work trends for 2026.”
  3. Iterate. Review the output, then ask follow‑up questions like “Add a bullet list of tools mentioned.” Claude will edit in place.
  4. Export. Click the three‑dot menu → “Copy to clipboard” or “Download as .txt”. Paste into your preferred editor.

4. Perplexity – AI‑Powered Research

Perplexity quickly finds concise answers from the web and cites sources.

  1. Open Perplexity. Go to perplexity.ai (free tier available).
  2. Ask a question. Type a query like “What are the latest GDPR updates in 2026?” and press Enter.
  3. Review citations. Each answer includes numbered source links. Click a number to view the original page.
  4. Save useful answers. Click the bookmark icon to add the response to your Perplexity library for later reference.

5. Todoist – AI‑Enhanced Task Management

Todoist now offers an AI assistant that can turn natural language into tasks.

  1. Create an account. Visit todoist.com and sign up for the free plan.
  2. Enable the AI assistant. In the sidebar, click “AI Assistant” and grant permission to read your projects.
  3. Add tasks by voice or text. Type or speak “Schedule a meeting with Alex next Tuesday at 10 am”. Todoist creates a task with the correct date and time.
  4. Prioritize automatically. Use the command “Prioritize my tasks for today”. The AI reorders items based on due dates and importance.

6. ChatGPT – General‑Purpose AI Companion

ChatGPT remains the most versatile assistant for brainstorming, coding help and quick answers.

  1. Log in. Go to chatgpt.com and sign in with your OpenAI account.
  2. Select the right model. For most productivity tasks, choose “GPT‑4” (if available) for higher quality output.
  3. Use system prompts. Begin with a brief instruction, e.g., “You are a freelance project manager. Help me draft a proposal for a client.”
  4. Export results. Click the three‑dot menu → “Copy” or “Download” to save the text.

7. Grammarly GO – AI Writing Coach

Grammarly GO goes beyond grammar checking by suggesting tone, style and even whole sentences.

  1. Install the extension. Download the Grammarly browser extension from grammarly.com.
  2. Activate GO. In the extension popup, toggle “GO” mode.
  3. Rewrite a paragraph. Highlight text in any web form, click the Grammarly icon, and choose “Rewrite”. Select a tone such as “Professional” or “Friendly”.
  4. Check for plagiarism. Click “Plagiarism” to ensure your content is original before publishing.

8. Motion – AI Calendar & Scheduling

Motion automatically finds optimal meeting times across multiple calendars.

  1. Sign up. Visit motion.ai and start a free trial.
  2. Connect calendars. In Settings → Calendar Integrations, link Google Calendar and Outlook.
  3. Set work preferences. Define your core hours (e.g., 9 am‑5 pm) and preferred meeting length.
  4. Schedule a meeting. Click “New Meeting”, add participants, and let Motion propose three time slots. Accept the one that fits.

9. Zapier – No‑Code Workflow Automation

Zapier lets you connect apps so that actions in one trigger actions in another.

  1. Create an account. Go to zapier.com and register for the free plan.
  2. Build a Zap. Click “Create Zap”, choose a Trigger app (e.g., Gmail → New Email), then an Action app (e.g., Todoist → Create Task).
  3. Map fields. In the Zap editor, drag email subject into the task title field.
  4. Test and turn on. Click “Test Zap” – you should see a new task appear in Todoist. Then toggle the Zap to “On”.

10. Superhuman – AI‑Enhanced Email

Superhuman adds AI shortcuts for sorting, replying and snoozing emails faster.

  1. Request an invite. Visit superhuman.com and fill the short form. A trial link arrives via email.
  2. Connect your inbox. Follow the on‑screen wizard to link Gmail or Outlook.
  3. Use AI shortcuts. Press “/” to open the command bar, type “Reply with summary” to let the AI draft a concise reply.
  4. Schedule follow‑ups. Highlight an email, press “Snooze”, and choose a future date – Superhuman will bring the message back to your inbox at the chosen time.

Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

  • Skipping the free tier. Test each tool on its free plan before committing to a paid subscription.
  • Over‑automating. Automate repetitive steps, but keep a manual review for important decisions.
  • Ignoring data privacy. Review each tool’s privacy policy; avoid uploading sensitive personal data to free services.
  • Not updating integrations. After a platform update (e.g., new Gmail UI), re‑authenticate the connection in Zapier or Motion.

FAQ

Do I need a powerful computer to run these AI tools?

All listed services run in the cloud. A modern web browser and an internet connection are enough.

Can I use these tools on mobile?

Yes. CraftNote, Notion, Todoist, and Superhuman all have iOS/Android apps. Zapier and Motion work in mobile browsers.

What if a tool’s free plan is too limited?

Most providers offer a trial or a low‑cost monthly tier. Start with the trial, measure the time saved, then decide if the cost is justified.

How do I keep my data safe?

Enable two‑factor authentication on each account, read the privacy sections on the provider’s website, and avoid storing confidential client information in free tiers unless the policy explicitly permits it.

Are these tools available worldwide?

All ten have global web access, but some features (e.g., payment methods) may be limited in certain countries. Check the provider’s “Availability” page for your region.
